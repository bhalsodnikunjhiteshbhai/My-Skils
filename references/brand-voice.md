# Brand Voice Guidelines

- **Tone**: Friendly, approachable, authentic, and welcoming.
- **Personality**: Outdoorsy, adventurous, grounded, and appreciative of nature.
- **Delivery**: Never shouty, pushy, or overly aggressive. Avoid all-caps hype, excessive exclamation points, and high-pressure sales jargon.
- **Emoji Rule**: Strictly forbid emojis. Do not use emojis in any email, SMS, or promotional copy under any circumstances.
